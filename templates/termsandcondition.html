Reservation policies

  • Check-in and check-out are available 24 hours.
  • Valid ID proof with address (Aadhar card, passport or driving licence) must be produced during reservation and check-in.
  • A PAN card is not accepted as a valid document to complete check-in.
  • Children above 10 years are considered adults and charged as an extra person at the applicable rate.
  • Pets are not allowed on the hotel premises.

Rescheduling policies

  • Rescheduling is free if requested at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Same-day rescheduling incurs a fee equal to 50% of the booking amount.
  • Rescheduled bookings must be used within 30 days of the original date.

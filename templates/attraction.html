Tips for a smoother visit.

A few things our guests are glad to know before heading out.

Start early

Sunrise at the viewpoint is best enjoyed before the crowds. Leave the lodge in the dark with a flask of tea.

Dress for temples

Wear modest, comfortable clothing and easy-to-remove footwear at temples. Phones and bags may be restricted inside.

Carry water and sun cover

The coast is bright and warm. A hat, sunscreen and a water bottle make a long day easier.

Let us arrange transport

Our travel desk can book a car and guide so you can see several places in one day.

Check the season

Waterfalls look best in and just after the monsoon, while winter is ideal for sightseeing.

Keep time for photos

Golden hour light on the sea and the Thiruvalluvar statue rewards a slower pace.
